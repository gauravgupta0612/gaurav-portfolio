I build the tools that take IBM i development, and the developers who do it, into the next decade.

For more than six years I have shipped software that real teams run every day: first enterprise CRM on Salesforce, then Java, Eclipse and VS Code tooling for IBM i at ARCAD Software, where I am a Senior Software Engineer and act as PMO for every R&D product.

My mission is simple: no developer should have to choose between a platform that runs the business and tools that feel modern. IBM i, z/OS and Java systems hold some of the world's most important data, and the people who look after them deserve editors that check code as they type, debuggers that just work, AI that protects their secrets, and deployments they can undo with one command.

Since 20 September 2026 I have designed, built and published 9 products and 36 public releases, from Vanthrex, a complete IBM i workbench for VS Code, to Tavrion Studio, an Eclipse-ready IDE for Windows. Each has a story in the Journal, and every installer is on the Downloads page.
